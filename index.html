Services

Hydro jetting or snaking?

Mechanical rodding pushes a bladed rod through a blockage. Jetting directs pressurized water at the debris. Which one fits depends on the obstruction and the pipe.

01

Hydro Jetting for Grease and Sludge

Mission's older kitchens, from Craftsman bungalows to mid-century homes, may sit on lines that have carried decades of cooking grease. A kitchen line that keeps slowing down may need more than a path punched through the blockage. Tell us about heavy kitchen use, food waste and how long the last cleaning held.

 02

Hydro Jetting for Tree Roots

Shary Heights preserved ebony trees when its lots were cleared in the 1940s, a reminder of how mature the trees on Mission's older streets are. Root trouble needs two answers: how to clear the line and whether the pipe has a defect that let the roots in. Tell us where roots were found and what happened afterward.

 03

Hydro Jetting for Recurring Clogs

A drain that keeps slowing in Mission may sit on a line from the 1920s citrus boom or from a recent subdivision. A drain that slows again after cleaning needs a fresh look. Note how long the improvement lasted and whether one fixture or several are affected.

 04

Hydro Jetting for Mineral Buildup

Hard deposits are a question about the pipe wall, so in Mission the first step is to identify the material. A slow drain does not mean a hard deposit is present. A qualified professional should identify the material and check the pipe before choosing a cleaning method.

 05

Preventative Hydro Jetting

Mission's historic houses have a long record to keep, and a written log helps most where the history is long. Plan cleaning around a record of past drain trouble, not a fixed rule that every pipe needs the same schedule. Keep notes on repeat blockages and any inspection findings.
